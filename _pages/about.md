Doumo ("Hey" in Japanese)!

Welcome to my personal page.

I am an independent ML researcher and an MSc student in Computer Science at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi. I am currently a Research Fellow in the [Fatima Fellowship](https://fatima.institute), mentored by Karim Farid (University of Freiburg), and an Analyst/Programmer at [Child Health and Mortality Prevention Surveillance (CHAMPS)](https://champshealth.org/) – ATBU. I completed my BSc in Computer Science at Al-Qalam University Katsina in 2023.

My research asks when neural networks genuinely generalize and when they only appear to, both in learning dynamics and in transfer to data-scarce settings, with applications in public health and environmental monitoring. Right now I study an interpolation regime that sits between memorization and generalization during training, and long-range transfer learning for water-quality prediction where local data is scarce.
